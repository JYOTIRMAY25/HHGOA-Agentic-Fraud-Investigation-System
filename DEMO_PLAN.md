# Demo Plan — 3 to 5 Minutes

## 1. Trigger
Show a suspicious transaction/risk signal entering the system.

## 2. Case creation
Show the agent opening/creating the investigation.

## 3. TigerGraph investigation
Show connected transactions, accounts, devices, identities, and prior cases.

## 4. GraphRAG
Show relevant policy, typology, and regulatory context.

## 5. Uncertainty
Show risk, confidence/uncertainty, and missing evidence.

## 6. Additional evidence
Show a controlled evidence request such as transaction validation or step-up authentication.

## 7. Reassessment
Show new evidence entering the case and the updated assessment.

## 8. Next-best action
Show recommendation, evidence, reason, and approval route.

## 9. Case memory
Show the final record and stored outcome for future investigations.

## 10. Benchmark
Show structured output for the 20 benchmark cases.

## Demo rule
Never fabricate evidence or claim a system result that was not actually retrieved.
